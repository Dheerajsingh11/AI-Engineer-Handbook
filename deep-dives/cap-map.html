Step 06The whole handbook, in one system

What the map shows

Every topic in the handbook appears above. The majority are Core — written directly into Atlas — which is the point: a single, realistic system genuinely exercises maths, Python, RAG, fine-tuning, agents, serving, evaluation, efficiency, safety and deployment together. The Foundations are the ideas underneath the code, the reason it works rather than merely runs. The Extensions are the honest edges — multimodal ingestion, a voice interface, multi-agent decomposition, GraphRAG — each a clear next step, and each already understood because its topic is in the handbook.

You have finished the handbook

From linear algebra to a deployed, evaluated, secured AI assistant — the distance is exactly what these twenty phases covered, and Atlas is the proof they connect. The remaining distance to the role is not more reading; it is building your own Atlas: pick a corpus you care about, run these chapters end to end, measure it honestly, deploy it, and write it up (Topic 17.4). That project — an experienced engineer shipping a real, measured AI system — is the whole job, and now you know how to build it.
